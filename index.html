Cutoff dates

The coloured period changeovers on your 2026 calendar are preloaded. Salary payment dates are different from the overtime cutoffs.

2026 dates were transcribed from the photograph. Later years are provisional, based on the last Sunday of each month, and must be checked before use. A cutoff is the last day included in that period. Changing it will affect period date ranges, but does not delete hours.

Backup & restore

Hours are stored in this browser, not on a server. Download a backup regularly, especially before changing phones or clearing browser data.
